# Attribution and evidence practice

The project integrates third-party platforms including ArduPilot/ArduCopter, Mission Planner, the Holybro X500/Pixhawk/PM02 hardware ecosystem, NVIDIA Jetson, and Samsung NVMe storage. The team does not claim authorship of those platforms.

Public technical references checked while preparing this snapshot:

- [ArduPilot AUTO mode](https://ardupilot.org/copter/docs/auto-mode.html)
- [ArduPilot companion computers](https://ardupilot.org/dev/docs/companion-computers.html)
- [ArduPilot vibration measurement](https://ardupilot.org/copter/docs/common-measuring-vibration.html)
- [ArduPilot power-monitor configuration](https://ardupilot.org/copter/docs/common-power-module-configuration-in-mission-planner.html)
- [NVIDIA Orin Nano developer-kit hardware](https://docs.nvidia.com/jetson/orin-nano-devkit/user-guide/latest/hardware_layout.html)
- [GitHub's guide to showcasing projects](https://docs.github.com/en/account-and-profile/tutorials/using-your-github-profile-to-enhance-your-resume)

Project-specific source records are named in the [evidence register](docs/evidence.md). Earlier AI-assisted flight analysis is identified as a derived report, not independently verified raw data.

Repository documentation and the offline summary utility were prepared with AI assistance. Engineering claims are separated into reviewed records, project-lead reports, and planned work. Future contributions should retain their actual author, test method, and supporting evidence.

No third-party source code, vendor manual pages, or product photographs have been copied into this initial package. No project-wide open-source license has been selected.
